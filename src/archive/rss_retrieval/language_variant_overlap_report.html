Google News Language Variant Overlap Experiment

Zika, fall armyworm, and avian influenza across 2005-2025 yearly windows

Author

INFLUX Impact

Published

April 1, 2026

1 Scope

This report compares Google News RSS overlap among requested language variants for:

  • Zika
  • Fall armyworm
  • Avian influenza

The experiment runs each topic across yearly windows from 2005 through 2025 for English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese language families. Within each family, the requested variants come from src/config/pipeline.yaml.

Overlap is computed on a stable article key derived from Google News article GUIDs or redirect URLs. The analysis is intentionally variant-first: it keeps requested variants separate so we can measure whether multiple variants add unique coverage beyond the best single variant.
